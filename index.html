About Student Engagement

The Office of Student Engagement at North University strives to provide students with organizations, leadership opportunities, and cultural relations that create a vibrant campus community. We aspire to foster an inclusive and welcoming environment where students feel welcome, become successful, and create lifelong friendships.

Our Services

  • Managing registration, funding and planning for campus activities and student clubs.
  • Providing resources such as campus food pantries, clothing closets, and more.
  • Hosting leadership development workshops and providing students with career support.
