Student Festival

Social

September 15, 2026 | 4:00 PM

Campus Quad

Join other North University students for our biggest social event of the fall semester. The Student Festival will have live music from student bands, many local food trucks, games, and booths from student organizations. It will be held on the campus quad. Bring your friends and have a great time!

About the Event

The Student Festival is North University's largest student run event, organized each fall by the Office of Student Engagement. It's designed to be a low pressure, enjoyable, and relaxing night where new and returning students can meet people, discover new clubs, and enjoy a break before midterms.

Attendee Expectations

The event begins at 4:00 PM on the quad and the festival runs until 9:00 PM. Expect a lively, outdoor, family-friendly atmosphere with live music, food, and games at several stations. The event is free and open to all North University students, staff, and their guests.

  • Live performances from student bands!
  • Food trucks featuring a variety of cuisines, free for students with valid ID!
  • Carnival games with prizes
  • Over 20 student organization booths to explore

Schedule

  • 4:00 PM - Event begins, food trucks and booths open
  • 5:00 PM - Student band performance
  • 6:30 PM - Carnival games open
  • 7:30 PM - Fireworks performance
  • 9:00 PM - Event concludes

Accessibility

The quad is fully accessible with paved pathways. Reserved seating is available near the main stage for any students or guests who need it. American Sign Language interpretation will be provided for the main stage performances. Service animals are welcome. Students or guests who need any additional accommodations can contact the Office of Student Engagement in advance at the email address listed in the footer.

Event Details

  • Date: September 15, 2026
  • Time: 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM
  • Location: Campus Quad
  • Organizer: Office of Student Engagement
  • Admission: Free for all North University students and guests
